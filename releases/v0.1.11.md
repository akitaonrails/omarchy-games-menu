# v0.1.11

Emulators and the installed companion tools now get proper 600x900 cover art
from SteamGridDB instead of the generic initial-letter placeholder. The bundled
catalog uses exact artwork titles for Azahar, Cemu, Dolphin, DuckStation, Eden,
Flycast, melonDS, PCSX2, RetroArch, RPCS3, shadPS4, xemu, Xenia Manager, 7th
Heaven Mod Manager, Eden Cheats Manager and ES-DE.

Catalog fragments that override a bundled entry without specifying
`sgdb_query` now inherit that one artwork field from the bundled catalog. This
keeps distrobox-gaming fragments authoritative for their declared metadata
without accidentally disabling bundled covers.

The lockfile also updates Tokio from the yanked 1.54.0 release to 1.54.1,
fixing RUSTSEC-2026-0384.
